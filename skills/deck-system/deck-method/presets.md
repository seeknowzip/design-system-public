# Purpose presets — story grammars

A preset fixes the **grammar** — archetype order, density, tone defaults — never the pixels. Per-slide composition still comes from each slide's spec. This is why several presets *reduce* slop rather than cause it: the "template rhythm" failure comes from one fixed layout reused regardless of content, while a grammar picks structure *from* content and purpose.

Slide numbers refer to `../deck-template/`. They are a menu, not a fixed order beyond the spine.

Density modes: **present** (the speaker completes the meaning; three-second glance) · **hybrid** (live, but must survive independent reading) · **read** (pre-read or leave-behind; labeled chunks, appendix).

> All presets are reusable story structures. Supply the project’s own evidence, voice and approved terms.

## 1 · `b2g-proposal` — public-sector proposal 

A public-sector proposal needs a verifiable record, policy fit, a delivery plan and a concrete request.

- **Spine:** Cover (01/09) → Agenda (10) → [chapter ×4: Divider (06) → content] → Ask (35/52) → Close (53)
- **Chapter grammar:** track record = proof (15/42), timeline (45), quote (30) / opportunity = evidence cards (16), comparison (21), data (17/43) / plan = flow (24/25), data (19), proof (42) / request = ask (52), roadmap (23)
- **Density:** hybrid — evidence, accountability, budget, and schedule must stand alone on screen.
- **Tone:** formal, plain, verifiable. Use the consuming project’s phrasing guide and confirmed terms.
- **Content chain:** identify the current factsheet, full proposal and approved slide plan in the brief; re-read them for each build. Do not treat a prior generated deck as the factual source.

## 2 · `sales-trip` — trip sales and partnerships 

- **Spine:** Brand cover (01) → customer context (11) → biggest value first: journey beats (51) → proof (42 record / 31 reviews) → CTA (52)
- **Cases:** brand cover with value proposition · 1-night-2-day journey beats · review quote · hero record number · inclusions/exclusions table (21) · partnership terms (26) · CTA with the accent on the CTA
- **Density:** present, with a leave-behind variant — same spine, heavier notes on evidence and terms.
- **Tone:** product voice — 존댓말 ("…해드려요"), benefit-led, honest about mechanism ("예약과 결제는 각 업체에 직접"). Vocabulary: 프로그램 / 대회 / 농구 여행 / 일정 / 신청 / 모집. Prices only when the rate is confirmed.

## 3 · `internal-strategy` — internal and board review

- **Spine:** Executive summary (41, answer first) → KPI and variance (49) → per issue [status → options (46) → recommendation] → risk (48) → decisions and owners (52)
- **Density:** read (pre-read). The meeting screen keeps only issues, options, and decisions.
- **Tone:** candid and non-defensive — good and bad together, blind spots and trade-offs named. Keep KPI definitions stable across meetings.
- **Rule:** minimize past reporting; most of the page goes to future issues and decisions.

## 4 · `investor` — investment pitch

- **Spine:** One-liner cover (54) → traction teaser (15) → problem (11 / 50 "now") → solution (50 "next") → traction detail (17/43) → market (47, bottom-up) → moat (46) → team (29) → use of funds and ask (52 + 20) → appendix
- **Density:** present plus appendix — 10–15 slides in the body, detail in the appendix.
- **Tone:** concrete and ambitious without overstatement. No vanity metrics, no cumulative-only figures, no dual axes, assumptions stated. Market sizing is bottom-up (reach × price); a standalone top-down TAM is banned.

## 5 · `keynote` — keynote and event opening

- **Spine:** Statement (53) → the audience's tension → now ↔ next alternating (50) → one evidence moment (15 or 30) → vision → memorable close (53)
- **Density:** minimal — one image, one line, or one number per slide. If it is better without a slide, don't use one.
- **Tone:** human and vivid. Rhythm over document completeness; detailed sources go in small captions or a separate handout.

## Choosing

| Situation | Preset | Density |
|---|---|---|
| Public-sector proposal or report | b2g-proposal | hybrid |
| Product sales or partnership pitch | sales-trip | present (+ leave-behind) |
| Internal decisions, quarterly review, partner board | internal-strategy | read |
| Investment or grant pitch | investor | present + appendix |
| Event opening, talk | keynote | minimal |
